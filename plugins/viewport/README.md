# Viewport

See and control the desktop of a self-hosted agent directly inside Buzz. Needs
**Remote Agents**.

## Getting started

1. Open a self-hosted agent and go to the **Desktop** tab.
2. Turn on **Desktop**. The agent gets its own virtual Linux desktop on your
   `buzz-host` machine, separate from your screen.
3. Open **Viewport** from the app dock (or choose **Open desktop**) to watch
   and control it inside Buzz.

## What it adds

- **In-app Viewport page:** view and control self-hosted agent desktops without
  leaving Buzz, with a collapsible agent Activity sidebar.
- **Desktop** tab on self-hosted agents: turn the desktop on or off and open it.
- **In-chat preview:** while the agent works on its desktop, a live still
  appears under its reply, with **Take over** and **Stop**.
- **Interactive takeover:** take over the mouse and keyboard, then hand control
  back to the agent.
